# Chrome Aura

The entire fragrance website with a BLACK + SILVER CHROME luxury aesthetic.

The website should feel like a high-end niche perfume brand mixed with modern luxury fashion and futuristic editorial design.

COLOUR PALETTE

Use a predominantly dark interface:

Jet Black: #050505

Deep Black: #0B0B0B

Charcoal: #151515

Graphite: #242424

Chrome Silver: #C8C8C8

Bright Silver: #E8E8E8

Dark Metallic: #707070

White: #F5F5F5

Do NOT use gold, beige, cream, pastel pink or warm colours as the primary interface colours.

The overall website should be approximately:

80% Black + 15% Silver/Chrome + 5% White

CHROME EFFECT

The silver elements should have a realistic chrome-metal finish, rather than simply being flat grey.

Use:

Metallic gradients

Glossy reflections

Subtle highlights

Dark-to-light silver transitions

Reflective chrome typography

Glass-like reflections

Very subtle glow around selected elements

The chrome should look like polished metal, not glitter.

HERO SECTION

Create a dramatic full-screen black hero section.

Place a large luxury perfume bottle in the centre with realistic reflective glass and chrome/silver details.

Use dramatic studio lighting with reflections on the bottle.

Heading:

"YOUR SCENT. YOUR SIGNATURE."

Use large elegant typography with a silver chrome finish.

Subheading:

"Luxury fragrances designed to leave an impression."

CTA buttons:

EXPLORE FRAGRANCES
SHOP COLLECTION

Buttons should have black backgrounds with thin chrome borders and a subtle metallic hover effect.

NAVIGATION

Create a minimal black navigation bar.

Logo:
Use a chrome silver logo.

Navigation:
HOME | SHOP | COLLECTIONS | OUR STORY | CONTACT

Right side:
SEARCH | ACCOUNT | WISHLIST | CART

Use thin silver lines and subtle chrome hover effects.

Keep the navigation extremely clean.

Heading:

"THE COLLECTION"

TYPOGRAPHY

Use a combination of:

Luxury Serif
for major headings and fragrance names.

Minimal Sans Serif
for body text, navigation and buttons.

Headings can have a subtle chrome gradient effect.

Keep typography spacious and editorial.

BACKGROUNDS & TEXTURES

Use:

Pure black

Glossy black

Matte black

Subtle graphite textures

Reflective chrome

Glass

Metallic surfaces

Avoid busy backgrounds.

Use negative space heavily.

ANIMATIONS

Animations should feel slow, smooth and expensive.

Use:

Smooth scrolling

Fade-in transitions

Product reveal animations

Chrome reflections moving across text

Subtle bottle rotation

Parallax photography

Slow image zoom

Metallic hover effects

Do NOT use excessive animations, neon effects or flashy transitions.

FOOTER

Create a completely black footer.

Use a chrome logo and thin silver divider lines.

Include:

SHOP
OUR STORY
CONTACT
FAQ
SHIPPING & RETURNS
PRIVACY POLICY
TERMS & CONDITIONS

Social icons should be silver/chrome.

FINAL VISUAL DIRECTION

The final website should feel like:

BLACK LUXURY + CHROME METAL + PERFUME + HIGH FASHION + FUTURISTIC EDITORIAL

Imagine the visual language of a luxury black perfume bottle, polished chrome, glossy glass, dark studio photography and high-fashion magazine campaigns.

Keep everything sophisticated and minimal.

The website should NEVER feel like a cheap cosmetics store or a generic Shopify template.

It should look like a premium fragrance house with a strong, recognizable visual identity.
